How we work

More than insurance

As a multi-agency, we work with many insurance companies, so we can compare their offers and help you choose the right policy. Our role does not end when the policy is signed. We are also here when you need help reporting and settling a claim, want to review your existing insurance or extend your cover to other areas of your life.

We value direct contact and an individual approach. You are not a policy number to us. We care about relationships built on trust, commitment and working together as partners.

Not sure where to start? Let's talk.

Whether you are looking for a specific policy or just weighing up the options, we will help you work out what you need and find a policy that gives you the right protection.

Write to us

  1. Review of current policies

    We will check the scope of cover, the sums insured and the exclusions.

  2. Comparing offers

    We will compare offers from several insurers, looking at the scope of cover, the terms and the price.

  3. Help with signing the policy

    We will explain the terms and help with the paperwork, so you know what the policy covers and how it works.

  4. Support with a claim

    We will help you report the claim, gather the documents and go through each stage of settlement.

  5. Ongoing care

    We will remind you when policies are due for renewal and help you judge whether your cover still fits your situation and needs.

About us

Polfinans Grupa PartnerskapolfinansGrupa Partnerska

Polfinans – Grupa Partnerska is a multi-agency insurance broker based in Wrocław that combines many years of experience, professional advice and an individual approach to every client. We have worked in the Polish market for 30 years with leading insurance companies. This lets us compare a wide range of offers and propose solutions that fit the needs of individuals, businesses and institutions.

We support you at every stage, from analysing your needs and choosing the right insurance, through signing the policy, to helping when a loss occurs and the claim is settled. We make sure the solutions we propose are clear, well chosen and match what our clients really need.

We value reliability, high-quality service and partnership. We believe good insurance is more than a policy. It is well-thought-out protection, peace of mind and the certainty that you can count on our support when it matters.

  • Informed decisionsWe explain the differences between offers and the reasons for our recommendations, so you know what you are choosing and why.
  • Individual approachYou know who is handling your case and who to ask if you have questions.
  • Ongoing supportWe are still here when a loss occurs or when your needs or circumstances change.
  • Continuous coverWe keep track of expiring policies and remind you about renewals, so your cover never lapses.
